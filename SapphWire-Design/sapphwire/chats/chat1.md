I have a great picture of the current SapphWire app — a network monitoring tool with 5 tabs (Graph, Usage, Things, Firewall, Alerts) currently in a flat dark gray theme. Let me design a pastel 2D cartoon version.

## My direction

I'm committing to a **"sticker book"** aesthetic:
- **Pastel palette**: cream background, mint/pink/lavender/peach/butter/sky accents — all share similar lightness/chroma
- **Thick ink outlines** (2.5px dark navy) on every card and chip — gives the cartoon look
- **Hard offset shadows** (no blur) like stickers pasted on paper
- **Slight rotations** on stickers and badges for handcrafted feel
- **Rounded, playful typography**: Fredoka for display, Nunito for body
- **Cartoon iconography**: chunky rounded SVG icons with the same ink stroke

I'll mock all 5 tabs as a fully interactive prototype with tab switching, time-pill toggles, hover states, and an animated live graph — the same data shape as the real app, but in the new theme.
